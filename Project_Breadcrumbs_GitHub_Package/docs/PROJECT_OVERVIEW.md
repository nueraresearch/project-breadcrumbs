# Project Breadcrumbs — Project Overview

**Organization:** Nuera Research and Developmental Laboratories  
**Tagline:** *Following patterns others miss.*  
**Document status:** Concept definition, Revision 1.0  
**Project status:** Proposal; no pilot launched

## 1. Executive summary

Project Breadcrumbs is a proposed community research initiative designed to better understand theft and recovery patterns through repeated deployments of low-cost, reusable monitoring packages.

Rather than focusing solely on individual incidents, the project seeks to examine patterns across deployments, including:
- when packages are disturbed, moved, or opened;
- where authorized device-location observations indicate a module has travelled;
- whether and when modules are recovered;
- how often modules can be reused;
- whether recurring patterns emerge across the observed sample.

The guiding principle is:

> **The goal isn't to catch people. The goal is to understand the system.**

The project is intended to study system-level patterns and evaluate whether a repeatable, community-scale data collection method produces useful observations. It is not designed to encourage confrontation, pursuit, or private enforcement.

## 2. Problem statement

Communities regularly discuss package theft, tool theft, fuel theft, property crime, and trespassing. Reports and anecdotes can describe individual incidents, but they may not provide enough consistent information to answer broader questions.

Examples of unanswered questions:
- Are observed incidents clustered by time or location?
- How often are packages moved without being opened?
- What proportion of deployed modules are recovered?
- How long does recovery take?
- Are observed outcomes consistent across deployment settings?
- Do the data support any useful, appropriately qualified patterns?

A small pilot cannot establish community-wide crime rates or prove causal relationships. It can assess feasibility, data quality, and whether a larger study is justified.

## 3. Research questions

1. Can a reusable module reliably record defined deployment and package-opening events?
2. Can a light-sensitive trigger detect opening-related light changes with an acceptable false-trigger rate?
3. Can the camera capture and store a three-image burst following a qualifying trigger?
4. What proportion of deployed modules are recovered within the defined recovery window?
5. What are the observed time intervals between deployment, event, and recovery?
6. Can location observations, where lawfully and technically available, support useful movement summaries?
7. What are the operational cost and maintenance burden per deployment cycle?
8. What privacy, safety, and participant concerns arise during voluntary deployment?

## 4. What Project Breadcrumbs is

A proposed community research program combining:
- reusable monitoring hardware;
- a defined deployment and recovery process;
- structured event and outcome records;
- a controlled data-handling process;
- analysis of aggregated deployment results.

The hardware concept includes a camera mounted inside the sealed package and aimed at the opening, a light-sensitive opening trigger inside the same cavity, movement sensing used for logging and location scheduling only, a unique module identifier, event storage sized to one deployment cycle, a rechargeable cell swapped at each cycle boundary, and a cellular link that transmits all records including images.

## 5. Why the name Breadcrumbs?

The name references the Hansel and Gretel story, in which breadcrumbs form a trail. Project Breadcrumbs uses the metaphor of event, movement, and recovery records forming a trail that may reveal patterns across multiple deployments.

## 6. Scope

### In scope
- Voluntary deployments by participating property owners on their own property.
- Reusable monitoring modules placed in packages for a defined research purpose.
- Detection and logging of defined package events.
- Three still images triggered by a qualifying light-change event, subject to approved privacy and legal review.
- Recovery and redeployment records.
- Aggregate analysis of deployment outcomes.

### Out of scope
- Encouraging confrontation, pursuit, or apprehension.
- Direct intervention by volunteers during an incident.
- Facial recognition, biometric identification, or automated identification of individuals.
- Audio recording or continuous video recording.
- Deployments on property without authorization.
- Claims that a device event proves theft, identity, intent, or guilt.
- Public release of participant identities, home addresses, or precise private-location traces. Event images are published, subject to the caption standard and location limits in the decision log.

## 7. Expected outputs

Potential outputs from a successful feasibility pilot:
- prototype reliability report;
- event-trigger performance and false-trigger results;
- image-capture performance report;
- module recovery and reuse statistics;
- data completeness assessment;
- operating-cost estimate;
- participant feedback summary;
- recommendations for redesign or a larger study.

## 8. Current status and limitations

Project Breadcrumbs is currently a proposal. No pilot has launched, no funding has been requested, and no participants are being recruited. The hardware design is conceptual. No component selection, performance figure, or cost estimate in this repository should be treated as validated unless explicitly supported by test evidence.
